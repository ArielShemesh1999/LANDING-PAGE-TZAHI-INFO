## 📄 What's on the Page

A single-page Hebrew (RTL) marketing landing page for a professional **painting & renovation business**. Designed for high conversion, fully responsive across desktop and mobile.

### Page Sections

- 🎨 **Hero** — Headline, sub-headline, and a bold call-to-action slogan
- 💚 **Floating CTA** — Sticky green pill button bridging hero and content
- ⭐ **Why Us** — Four key differentiators (experience, warranty, cleanliness, on-time delivery)
- 🛠️ **How It Works** — Three-step process (Prep → Paint → Clean)
- 📞 **Phone Strip** — Direct contact line for quick calls / WhatsApp
- ➕ **Additional Services** — Mold treatment, decorative paint, drywall, renovation
- ❓ **Instant Quote Quiz** — Three-question form for live price estimation
- 💰 **Pricing Table** — Transparent pricing by room count and condition
- ✅ **What's Included** — Bullet list of items covered in every base price
- 📝 **Contact Form** — Name, phone, city, rooms, furnished status
- 🏆 **Benefits Grid** — Six brand promises (no deposits, written agreement, transparent pricing, etc.)
- 💬 **FAQ** — Five frequently asked questions
- 🔗 **Footer** — Contact info and social links
